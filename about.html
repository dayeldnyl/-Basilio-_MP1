Let's Talk

About AllBlack

One color. Ten everyday essentials. Built for people who prefer clean design over visual noise.

Our Story

AllBlack started in 2026 with a simple annoyance: finding everyday gear that actually matches. A bright logo on a tumbler, a glossy plastic lamp on a clean desk, or a mismatched umbrella can throw off an otherwise simple setup.

Instead of building a massive store full of trends that fade in a month, we narrowed our focus to ten staple items people reach for every day—reimagined in deep matte, obsidian, and jet-black finishes.

Mission & Philosophy

Intentional Simplicity

We strip away flashy graphics and unnecessary details so each product focuses on function and clean form.

Unified Aesthetic

Every finish is selected to complement the rest of the lineup, from workspace tools to daily carry items.

Built for Daily Use

Minimalist design should never feel fragile. We prioritize durable materials that hold up to everyday routines.

The Founder

Founder & Creative Director

Daniel Basilio

Daniel founded AllBlack with the goal of making minimalist, monochrome gear easy to find in one place. Combining an interest in clean visual design with practical business sensibilities, he handpicked the initial ten-item lineup to cover workspace, travel, and wardrobe basics without compromise.
